Programs

What we run.

Our four core outreach programs.

PROGRAM 01

FLL Coaching

We coach nine elementary and middle school FIRST LEGO League teams every year, providing meeting space, mentors, and parts.

PROGRAM 02

STEM Nights

Hands-on demos and mini-builds at partner schools across the Roseville Joint Union district.

PROGRAM 03

Summer Camp

A one-week intro-to-robotics camp for rising 6th–8th graders, taught entirely by our students.

PROGRAM 04

Community Demos

Robot demos at city events, library makerspaces, and corporate partner open houses.
