What we do

Six subteams. One robot.

Every student picks a specialty — and crosses over often. The team works the way a small engineering firm works: parallel disciplines, weekly demos, shared deadlines.

01

Mechanical

CAD-first design, CNC and 3D-printed prototypes, then competition-grade fabrication using aluminum, polycarb, and carbon fiber.

SolidWorks · CNC · Lathe
02

Electrical

Wire harnesses, motor controllers, sensors, and pneumatics — built clean, labeled, and serviceable in under five minutes.

REV · CTRE · Pneumatics
03

Software

Java command-based control, computer vision with Limelight, path planning, and a custom dashboard for the driver station.

Java · OpenCV · WPILib
04

Strategy & Scouting

Match analysis, pick-list modeling, and live data collection at competitions — informing every alliance decision.

Data · Match Film · Picklist
05

Business & Awards

Sponsor relations, the impact award submission, branding, social media, and the team's annual essay and presentation.

Comms · Brand · Awards
06

Outreach

FLL coaching, STEM nights, summer camps, and demos across Granite Bay and the greater Sacramento region.

FLL · STEM · Community
