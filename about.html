About the team

Students engineering students.

We're a student-run VRC Robotics Competition team based out of Granite Bay High School. Our mission is to make competitive engineering accessible — and to graduate teammates who can build, lead, and think.

Mission

Build robots. Build people. Build community.

VRC changed our lives. Our job is to pay that forward — by designing a competitive robot every season, mentoring younger teams, and reaching every student in our community who's curious about how things work.

Values
  • Students decide. Mentors coach; students drive every technical and business decision.
  • Document everything. If it isn't written down, it didn't happen.
  • Gracious professionalism. We compete hard and help generously.
  • No spectators. Every member ships something every week.
